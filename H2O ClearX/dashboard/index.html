02 / THE MEASUREMENT

A beam through
the unknown.

A red light source crosses the sample tube. Clear water transmits more light; suspended particles scatter it away from the photodiode. The ESP32 averages ten ADC samples, classifies the voltage, and estimates a relative concentration against your calibrated baseline.
